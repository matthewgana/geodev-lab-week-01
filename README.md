# Spatial Market Accessibility Analysis

**GeoDev Lab Cohort 1 — 2026**
**Week 01 Project**

**Author:** MATTHEW GANA

## Project Brief

### Spatial Question

Which settlements in Minna Metropolitan Area are within 5 km of a major road but more than 10 km from a mapped market?

### Study Area

Minna Metropolitan Area, Niger State, Nigeria

### Datasets

* **Administrative boundaries:** GRID3 — https://grid3.org/geospatial-data-nigeria
* **Settlement extents:** GRID3 — https://grid3.org/geospatial-data-nigeria
* **Road network:** OpenStreetMap / Geofabrik — https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html
* **Market locations:** OpenStreetMap — https://www.openstreetmap.org/

### Analysis

The project will identify settlements within 5 km of a major road and more than 10 km from a mapped market.
