# Project Brief

**Author:** MATTHEW GANA

## 1. Spatial Question

Which settlements in Minna Metropolitan Area are within 5 km of a major road but more than 10 km from a mapped market?

## 2. Study Area

Minna Metropolitan Area, Niger State, Nigeria

## 3. Datasets

| Dataset                                  | Purpose                 | Source                                            |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| GRID3 Nigeria Operational LGA Boundaries | Define the study area   | https://grid3.org/geospatial-data-nigeria         |
| GRID3 NGA — Settlement Extents v4.1      | Identify settlements    | https://grid3.org/geospatial-data-nigeria         |
| OpenStreetMap Road Network — Nigeria     | Identify major roads    | https://download.geofabrik.de/africa/nigeria.html |
| OpenStreetMap Market Features            | Identify mapped markets | https://www.openstreetmap.org/                    |

**Market extraction:** QGIS QuickOSM

## 4. Proposed Analysis

Identify settlements that are:

* Within 5 km of a major road
* More than 10 km from a mapped market

The analysis will be conducted within the defined study area.
